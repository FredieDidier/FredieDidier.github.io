About

I am a doctoral student in Economics at the Brazilian Institute of Education, Development and Research (IDP).

I work on applied economics, with a focus on health, climate, labor, and crime.

I hold an MRes in Economics from the University of Warwick and a BA in Economics from PUC-Rio. Alongside my doctoral work I am a researcher at Cidacs (Fiocruz/Bahia), where I take part in the Cidacs Clima project, and at the Bahia Peace Observatory, where I study lethal violence against Black and peripheral youth. I also work as a data analyst at Quaest.
